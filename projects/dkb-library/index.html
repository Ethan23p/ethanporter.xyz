I didn't trust modern AI models to capture the full nuance of a given article or whitepaper, let alone a body of research, so I made an agentic system that sits atop my research and serves as my librarian, subject-matter expert, and data scientist.

This project was first shipped as my entry to a competition hosted by the Future of Life Foundation; now I've archived it as I develop a more general solution.

What I built: An AI system that reads, retrieves, and synthesizes information across a body of research. It maintains the source data verbatim, then enables your AI agent to be a dutiful librarian, a subject-matter expert, or a data scientist, on demand.
