I wanted an app that provided insight, validation, and confidence, like horoscopes do for some people, but based on data and personality psychology, instead of constellations.

How it works

  • Assessment. Short, quirky scenarios with four responses each. Every scenario pairs two of the Big Five traits, and each response nudges the profile toward one combination of them.
  • Profile. Five trait scores between -1 and 1. Each response moves them a little less as the profile settles, and recent responses outweigh old ones, so it follows a person as they change.
  • Guidance. A short reframing aimed at the user, written by Gemini from their latest profile and the guidances they have already had.
